OUR STORY

Church History

Baptist International Worship Centre (BIWC) is a church family in Asokwa, Kumasi, Ghana. Our story is shaped by a desire to worship God, remain grounded in the Bible and share the love of Jesus Christ with people from every walk of life. From the beginning, we have sought to be a church for our time, carrying a lasting zeal for God and a warm welcome for all who come through our doors.

Over the years, BIWC has grown through worship, prayer, teaching and fellowship. Sunday Elevation Service, Word Digest, Friday PHD Service and our online Morning Devotion, Mercy Hour and Boot Camp give the church family regular opportunities to gather, learn from God's Word and encourage one another. Our ministries and community life also make room for people to build relationships and serve with their gifts.

As we look toward our tenth anniversary in 2027, we give thanks for God's faithfulness and for everyone who has been part of the journey. We continue to grow in faith and reach out to our community with hope, love and a commitment to serve. Wherever you are in your journey, there is a place for you at BIWC.

Our Mission

To worship God, make disciples through His Word and serve people in the love of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To be a welcoming, growing church family with a 21st-century presence and a 1st-century zeal for God.

Our Beliefs

We believe in God and in Jesus Christ, and look to the Bible as God's Word and our guide for faith and life. We value prayer, worship, fellowship and sharing the good news of Christ.

GROW IN FAITH

Frequently Asked
Spiritual Questions

Explore a few common questions about faith, prayer and following Jesus.

How can I begin a relationship with God?

Begin by turning to God in faith. The Bible teaches that we are saved by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by earning it through good works (Ephesians 2:8–9). You can speak honestly with God, ask for His forgiveness and trust Jesus as your Saviour.

What does it mean to follow Jesus?

Following Jesus means trusting Him, learning from His teachings and seeking to live with love, humility and obedience to God. It is a lifelong journey of growing in faith (Luke 9:23).

How do I pray?

Prayer is talking with God. You can thank Him, share what is on your heart, ask for help and pray for others. Jesus taught His followers to pray with sincerity and trust (Matthew 6:9–13).

Why should I read the Bible?

The Bible helps us know God, understand the good news of Jesus and find guidance for daily life. You could begin with one of the Gospels, such as Mark or John, and read a little at a time (2 Timothy 3:16–17).

Why is being part of a church important?

Church gives us a place to worship, learn from God's Word, encourage one another and serve together. Christians are encouraged to keep meeting and building one another up (Hebrews 10:24–25).

Can I ask someone at BIWC for prayer or guidance?

Yes. We would be glad to listen and pray with you. Visit our prayer page or contact us to get in touch.
